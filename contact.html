Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers to Common Queries

How do I book a taxi or tempo traveler?

You can book our services by calling us directly, filling out the contact form on this page, or sending us a WhatsApp message. We'll confirm availability and provide you with a quote.

What is your cancellation policy?

For local bookings, cancellations made 24 hours before the scheduled time are eligible for a full refund. For outstation bookings and tour packages, cancellations made 48 hours in advance receive a full refund. Late cancellations may incur charges.

Do you provide drivers who speak English?

Yes, we have drivers who are proficient in English, Hindi, and some other regional languages. If you need an English-speaking driver, please mention it while making your booking.

Can I customize a tour package?

Absolutely! We offer fully customizable tour packages tailored to your preferences, group size, and time constraints. Just let us know your requirements, and we'll design a package specifically for you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers, and digital payment methods including UPI, credit/debit cards, and popular wallet apps. For advance bookings, we require a partial payment to confirm your reservation.
