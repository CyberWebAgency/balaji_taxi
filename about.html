About Balaji Taxi - Mathura's Leading Cab Service

Your Trusted Travel Partner in Mathura & Vrindavan Since 2016

Balaji Taxi & Tempo Traveler, established in 2016, is a premier taxi service provider located in the holy land of Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. We started as a small family business with just two vehicles and have now grown into one of the most trusted transportation service providers in the Mathura-Vrindavan region with a fleet of 25+ well-maintained vehicles.

We specialize in providing comfortable and reliable taxi services for pilgrims, tourists, and locals visiting Mathura, Vrindavan, and surrounding areas of Braj region. Whether you need a cab for visiting sacred temples, planning a family vacation, or require a tempo traveler for group travel, our local drivers provide safe and convenient transportation solutions.

What sets Balaji Taxi apart is our deep knowledge of Mathura-Vrindavan's local culture, religious sites, and hidden gems. Our experienced drivers are locals who provide valuable insights that enhance your travel experience and help you connect with the spiritual essence of this sacred land. We offer competitive rates, clean vehicles, and 24/7 availability to ensure a hassle-free journey in the land of Lord Krishna.

Our Mission

To provide safe, comfortable, and affordable taxi service in Mathura while delivering exceptional customer experience. We aim to exceed expectations through reliability, punctuality, and personalized service for every visitor to the holy city of Mathura.

Our Vision

To become the leading taxi and tempo traveler service provider in the Braj region, known for excellence, integrity, and customer satisfaction. We aspire to continually grow our fleet of cabs and tempo travelers while maintaining our commitment to quality transportation services.

The Journey of Balaji Taxi Mathura

How We Became Mathura's Preferred Cab Service

2016

Humble Beginnings in Mathura

Balaji Taxi & Tempo Traveler started with just two vehicles - a sedan and a tempo traveler. Operating from a small office in Mathura, we began offering local taxi services to pilgrims visiting the holy city's temples and ghats.

2017

Expansion of Taxi Services

Our commitment to quality service led to rapid growth. We expanded our fleet to 10 vehicles and started offering outstation taxi services from Mathura to Delhi, Agra, and other nearby cities. We also introduced our first customized temple tour packages for the Braj region.

2018

Recognition as Mathura's Top Taxi Service

We were recognized as one of the best taxi service providers in Mathura by the local tourism department. Our customer base grew significantly with positive reviews, and we started partnerships with hotels and travel agencies in Mathura-Vrindavan region.

2019

Modernization of Our Cab Service

We invested in technology by launching our website and implementing a digital booking system for our taxi service. Our fleet expanded to include luxury vehicles for premium car rental, and we established a dedicated customer support team to ensure 24/7 service for Mathura visitors.

2022

Today's Balaji Taxi Mathura

Today, we operate a fleet of over 25 vehicles ranging from sedans to luxury tempo travelers serving thousands of pilgrims and tourists in Mathura. We continue to innovate and improve our taxi services while staying true to our core values of reliability, safety, and customer satisfaction.
