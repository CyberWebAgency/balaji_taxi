About Our Taxi Service in Mathura

Your Trusted Cab Service in Mathura Since 2016

Balaji Taxi & Tempo Traveler is a premier transportation service provider in the holy land of Mathura and Vrindavan, dedicated to offering comfortable, reliable, and affordable travel solutions for tourists, pilgrims, and locals alike.

Starting as a small family business, we have grown into one of the most trusted taxi and tempo traveler service providers in Mathura region, serving thousands of pilgrims and tourists visiting the sacred sites of Braj Mandal including Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan, Gokul, and Barsana. Our fleet of well-maintained vehicles and team of professional drivers ensure a safe and comfortable journey for all our customers.
