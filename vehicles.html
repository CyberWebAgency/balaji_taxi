Frequently Asked Questions about Our Vehicles

Common queries answered for your convenience

What types of vehicles do you offer for rent in Mathura?

We offer a wide range of vehicles including hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and Tempo Travelers with seating capacities ranging from 4 to 20 passengers. Our fleet includes popular models like Swift Dzire, Toyota Innova, Maruti Ertiga, and various Tempo Traveler options.

Do you provide drivers with all vehicles?

Yes, all our vehicles come with professional, experienced drivers who are familiar with Mathura, Vrindavan, and surrounding areas. They are well-trained, polite, and focused on providing you with a safe and comfortable journey.

What is the pricing structure for vehicle rentals?

Our pricing depends on the vehicle type, rental duration, and whether it's a local or outstation trip. We offer both hourly packages and full-day rentals. Sedans typically start at ₹1500 per day, SUVs at ₹2500 per day, and Tempo Travelers at ₹4500 per day. Please contact us for a customized quote based on your requirements.

Can I book a vehicle for outstation trips from Mathura?

Absolutely! We specialize in outstation trips from Mathura to destinations like Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Haridwar, and other popular locations. We offer both one-way and round-trip options with experienced drivers who are familiar with these routes.

Do you offer airport pickup and drop services?

Yes, we provide reliable airport pickup and drop services to/from Delhi Airport, Agra Airport, and other nearby airports. Our drivers track flight timings and ensure they are at the airport on time, even if your flight is delayed.
