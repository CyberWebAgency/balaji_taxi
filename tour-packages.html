Braj 84 Kos Yatra

The Complete Spiritual Circuit

The Sacred 84 Kos Yatra

The 84 Kos Parikrama (approximately 252 km) is considered one of the most sacred journeys for devotees of Lord Krishna. This circuit covers all the places in the Braj Mandal where Lord Krishna is believed to have performed his divine pastimes.

Our 7-day package allows you to complete this spiritual journey with comfortable transportation, accommodation, meals, and experienced guides who narrate the religious significance of each location.

Key Features:

  • Visit all major shrines and temples in the Braj circuit
  • Comfortable accommodation throughout the journey
  • Vegetarian meals included
  • Experienced guides with deep knowledge of Krishna legends
  • Air-conditioned vehicle for transportation

This package can be customized based on your time availability and specific places of interest.
